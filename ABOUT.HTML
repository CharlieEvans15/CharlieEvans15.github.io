About Our Shop

We are situated in the historic centre of Lincoln. Common Grounds is located only a stone's throw away from the Cathedral, in a parade of shops just off Steep Hill. The building has roots stretching all the way back to Lincoln's Roman past.

The charm of steep Hill and its connected streets has not gone unnoticed. It has been awarded numerous awards and accolades over the years. Most recently, it was awarded the historic center in 2022.

Situated in a building which has been dated to medieval times, the surroundings and ambience of coffee grounds certainly adds to the feeling of being in Lincoln's Cathedral quarter.

The Owners

The owners are Mary and Adam. They started this business with a passion for quality coffee and a commitment to creating a welcoming space for the community.

They wanted to create a place where people could come together, share ideas, and enjoy great coffee in a warm and inviting atmosphere.

This is a family business that makes quality coffee with care and dedication.
